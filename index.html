Privacy Policy for Promotion Predictor

Effective date: 01 Oct 2026
Last updated: 01 Oct 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how Promotion Predictor ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use the application. Promotion Predictor is an independent, unofficial tool built for Sindh College Education staff and is not published by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any government department.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1.Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to all users of the Promotion Predictor Android application, available on Google Play.

2.Information the App accesses

The App is designed to work with a seniority-list workbook (a spreadsheet in .xlsx format) that determines the promotion simulation. This workbook may contain the following categories of information about the officers listed in it:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Seniority number
  • Subject / department
  • Merit position and year of appointment
  • Recruitment type and case-status flags used for administrative tracking

This workbook is not collected by us. It is either:

  1. fetched directly by your own device from a Google Sheets link that you or your organization configures, or
  2. selected manually by you from local storage on your device.

In both cases, the transfer of this file happens directly between your device and the storage location you choose (e.g., Google's servers, or your device's own file system). We do not operate, control, or have access to any server that receives, stores, or logs this file or its contents.

3.How information is processed

All promotion-simulation calculations are performed entirely on your device, using the on-device processor. The App does not transmit the contents of the seniority workbook — including any names, dates of birth, or other personal information within it — to us or to any third party.

4.Local storage and caching

To avoid re-downloading the workbook every time you open the App, a copy of the processed data is cached in the App's private, sandboxed storage area on your device (an area of storage that other apps cannot access). This cached copy:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is automatically and permanently deleted if you uninstall the App or clear the App's storage/data from your device settings

5.Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the App does not transmit to us:

  • The contents of any seniority workbook (names, dates of birth, or any other officer data)
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Contacts, camera, microphone, or call/SMS data
  • Financial or payment information
  • Any account credentials (the App does not require account creation, sign-in, or authentication of any kind)

6.Third-party services

Google Sheets / Google Drive: If the App's configured data source is a Google Sheets link, your device connects directly to Google's servers to download that file over a standard encrypted (HTTPS) connection. This connection is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not this one. We do not receive any data from, or insert any tracking into, this connection.

No analytics or advertising: The App does not integrate any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDK of any kind. We do not serve ads, and no third party can use the App to serve ads to you.

7.Permissions requested

The App may request storage/file access permission on your device. This permission is used solely to allow you to select a seniority workbook file for the App to read. It is never used to access, scan, or transmit any other file on your device.

8.Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any information with third parties, because we do not collect or receive any information from the App in the first place.

9.Data retention and deletion

Since we do not collect or store any of your data on any server we operate, there is no data for us to retain or for you to request deletion of from us. All data related to your use of the App exists only on your own device, in the App's private storage, and is removed automatically when you uninstall the App. You may also clear this data at any time via your device's Settings → Apps → Promotion Predictor → Storage → Clear Data, without uninstalling the App.

10.Children's privacy

The App is an administrative planning tool intended for use by adult academic staff and administrators. It is not directed at, marketed to, or knowingly used by children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and — per Section 5 above — the App does not collect personal information from any user, regardless of age.

11.Data security

Because your data is processed and stored exclusively on your own device and is never transmitted to us, its security depends primarily on your own device's security (e.g., screen lock, device encryption) and on Google's security practices for the Google Sheets connection described in Section 6. We use standard Android platform mechanisms (app-private sandboxed storage) to ensure the cached copy on your device is not accessible to other applications.

12.Your choices and controls

  • You control which seniority workbook, if any, the App loads.
  • You can clear all locally cached data at any time via your device settings, as described in Section 9.
  • You can revoke the App's storage/file access permission at any time via your device settings, which will prevent the App from loading a new workbook file until permission is granted again.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all data associated with it from your device.

13.International users

The App does not transmit your data anywhere, including across borders, since all processing happens locally on your device. If you use the Google Sheets loading feature, the location of Google's servers is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

14.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
